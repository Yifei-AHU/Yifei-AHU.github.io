## About Me

🧑‍🔬 I am currently an **Assistant Researcher at Anhui University**. My research interests include **cross-modal person retrieval**, **multimodal visual grounding**, and **embodied search**. I am particularly interested in multimodal learning and vision-language understanding for target retrieval and localization in complex real-world environments.

🎓 I received my Ph.D. from **Anhui University**, where I conducted my research under the guidance of **Prof. Chenglong Li** and **Prof. Jin Tang**. During my doctoral studies, my research mainly focused on text-image cross-modal retrieval, aerial vision, and cross-view multimodal representation learning.

🔍 My long-term research goal is to develop intelligent perception systems that can progressively move from **passive retrieval and localization** toward **active embodied target search and interaction**.

## Research Interests

- **Low-Altitude Multi-modal Visual Perception:** Cross-modal Person Retrieval, RGBT Visual Grounding, RGBT Semantic Segmentation
- **Embodied AI:** Low-Altitude Embodied Search, Embodied Navigation
